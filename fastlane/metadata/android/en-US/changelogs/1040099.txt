Sonic opens a port on your router (NAT-PMP or UPnP), so phones at home can reach each other. Settings shows whether others can reach you, how to forward the port by hand, and when your provider blocks it (CGNAT). Uploads to people Sonic can't reach go through the server's relay. Chat has a "browse their shelf" key, and a shelf that won't open says why. All ten Friday Drops are new every week.
